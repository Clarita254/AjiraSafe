ABOUT AJIRASAFE

Helping job seekers navigate online opportunities safely.

AjiraSafe is designed to help job seekers make more informed decisions by providing job risk assessment, employer verification information and understandable explanations before they apply.

OUR PURPOSE

A safer way to assess online jobs.

Online recruitment provides access to many employment opportunities, but job seekers may also encounter advertisements containing misleading information, suspicious requests or other potential warning signs.

AjiraSafe brings job advertisement analysis and employer verification together to provide useful information that can support a more informed decision before applying.
